Sean's Story

A few months back, Sean experienced a transient ischemic attack and lost his vision for weeks. He then learned he had an undiagnosed heart condition. He had been on the mend and making significant strides toward recovery when, suddenly, on August 14th, Sean suffered a stroke.

He is in Portland, Maine, receiving excellent care from his medical providers, and is supported by his parents, sister, fiancée, and close family and friends. As this community rallies around him, every message and every bit of help brings him closer to healing.
